03 ABOUT THE PORTAL

INTRODUCING LUPONRESOLVE

A digital approach to barangay dispute resolution.

LuponResolve is a barangay dispute-resolution and case management portal designed to make community services more accessible to residents and more organized for authorized barangay personnel.

Through the platform, residents can submit complaints online and monitor case progress using their tracking credentials, while authorized personnel can access dedicated tools to review and manage submitted cases.

01

Accessibility

Providing residents with convenient digital access to essential barangay services.

02

Organization

Supporting a structured approach to complaint submissions and case management.

03

Transparency

Helping residents stay informed through accessible case-status updates.
